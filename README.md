# Questions You MUST Ask Before Installing Commercial Solar

Before committing to a solar installation, ask these critical questions.

### 1. What Size Solar System Does My Business Actually Need?

The right system depends on:

- Electricity consumption  
- Peak demand  
- Operational hours  
- Roof space  

A warehouse, factory, or office park may require **30kW, 50kW, or even 100kW solar installations** depending on energy demand.

---

### 2. How Will the System Reduce My Electricity Costs?

A professional installer should clearly explain:

- Expected monthly savings  
- Return on investment timeline  
- Solar vs Eskom cost comparisons  

Solar should be positioned as a **financial investment — not just an energy upgrade**.

---

### 3. Will the System Work During Load Shedding?

Solar systems can provide power during load shedding when properly designed with:

- battery storage  
- load management  
- correct inverter configuration  

This is critical for **business continuity in South Africa**.

---

### 4. Who Will Handle System Commissioning?

Commissioning ensures the system:

- Produces expected energy output  
- Operates safely  
- Meets engineering specifications  

This step is often skipped by inexperienced installers.
